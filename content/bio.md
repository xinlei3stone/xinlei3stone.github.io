I am a master's student in Materials Science and Engineering at the University of Science and Technology Beijing (class of 2027). In 2027 I will join **SMIC South (中芯南方)** as a **Yield Enhancement Engineer**. Yield enhancement is where process detail meets statistics — finding the defect and variation signatures that limit wafer yield, tracing them to root cause with the module teams, and turning the fix into a controlled process change. I am preparing for that work through statistics, DOE/SPC, and the data tools I build for myself.

- **A failure-analysis mindset.** SEM/TEM, XRD/XPS/Raman, and electrochemical diagnostics — separating intrinsic degradation from test artifacts instead of stopping at the number.
- **Scale-up exposure.** Wet-chemical process design taken from the lab to 1.8 m × 1.8 m electrodes, with accelerated durability testing and industry partners.
